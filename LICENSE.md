### ⚠️ One important thing about the form

GitHub Pages **cannot process form submissions** — it's static hosting only. The form above will render and look correct, but clicking "Submit" won't send anything until you point `action="#"` at a real endpoint. The README explains exactly how to fix that with Formspree (free, 2-minute setup). Everything else — layout, styling, phone links, email links, responsiveness — works immediately with zero configuration.
